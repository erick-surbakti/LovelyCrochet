## Who we are

Simpul Mungil is a home crochet business in Bekasi, West Java, Indonesia. For anything about your personal data, contact us at purnamaw689@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +62 812-9676-1659.

## What we collect

- **When you order on WhatsApp:** your name, WhatsApp number, delivery address, order details and proof of payment.
- **When you sign up for updates:** your email address and, if you provide it, your WhatsApp number.
- **When you visit the website:** anonymous visit statistics (see Analytics). We don't use tracking cookies.

We never ask for or store credit card details. You pay directly by QRIS, bank transfer or your own e-wallet.

## How we use it

- To process, make and ship your order.
- To contact you about your order, such as design approval, payment and tracking numbers.
- To send updates and free patterns, only if you signed up.
- To meet legal obligations, such as keeping transaction records.

We never sell, rent or share your data for anyone else's marketing.

## Who receives your data

We only share what's necessary with:

- **Couriers** (JNE, J&T, SiCepat, AnterAja, GoSend, GrabExpress): recipient name, phone number and address for delivery.
- **Our form provider** (Netlify Forms): the email and WhatsApp number from the newsletter sign-up form.
- **Marketplaces** (if you buy through one): your data is handled under that marketplace's privacy policy.

## Analytics

If analytics is switched on, we use a cookie-free analytics service that collects no personal data and doesn't track you across websites. We only see aggregate numbers, such as page visits and WhatsApp button clicks.

## How long we keep data

- Order data: kept for 5 years for transaction records, then deleted.
- Newsletter data: kept until you unsubscribe.
- WhatsApp chat history: follows WhatsApp's own storage. You can ask us to delete it.

## Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:

- know what data we hold about you,
- request a copy of it,
- ask us to correct inaccurate data,
- ask us to delete data that's no longer needed,
- withdraw consent to receiving updates at any time.

Send your request by email or WhatsApp. We'll respond within 3 working days.

## Security

This website uses HTTPS. Order data is kept on locked devices accessed only by the business owner. In the event of a data breach, we will notify you as required by law.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date of the latest update is always shown at the top of this page.

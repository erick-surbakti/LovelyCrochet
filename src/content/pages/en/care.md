## Golden rules for all crochet

1. **Cool or room-temperature water.** Hot water shrinks cotton and felts wool.
2. **Press, don't wring.** Wringing twists the fibres and distorts the shape.
3. **Dry flat in the shade.** Never hang it wet, and keep it out of strong sun.
4. **No bleach, no tumble dryer.**
5. **Store somewhere dry** with silica gel, because Indonesia is humid.

Want step-by-step instructions? Read [how to care for crochet](/en/journal/how-to-care-for-crochet/).

## Care by yarn type

| Yarn | Washing | Drying | Storage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Milk cotton | Hand-wash, cool water, mild soap | Flat on a towel, in the shade | Folded or upright, with silica gel | May pill slightly. Use a fabric shaver. |
| Baby milk cotton | Hand-wash with baby soap; wash before first use | Flat, in the shade | Clean fabric bag | Rinse until completely soap-free. |
| 100% cotton | Hand-wash, cool water | Flat; hats over an upturned bowl | Folded | Can shrink in hot water. Softens with washing. |
| Acrylic | Hand-wash, or a gentle machine cycle in a mesh bag | Flat, dries quickly | Folded | Never iron directly. Distant steam is fine. |
| Cotton-poly | Hand-wash, cool water | Upright with a towel inside (bags) | Stuffed with paper to hold shape | Very strong. Reshape while damp. |
| Wool | Hand-wash, cool water, wool wash, no rubbing | Flat, away from heat | With mothballs or cedar | Warm water and rubbing cause felting. |

## Care by product

### Crochet bags

- Don't overfill. Heavy items belong in a lined bag.
- Stuff with paper in storage.
- Dab small stains with a damp cloth. Don't scrub.
- After washing, dry upright or lying down, never hanging by the straps.

### Amigurumi

- Wipe the surface with a damp cloth for light dirt.
- Wash children's toys thoroughly every 1–2 months.
- Make sure the stuffing is completely dry (1–2 days) so it doesn't go mouldy.
- Reshape ears and arms while damp.

### Crochet bouquets

- **Don't soak**, because the stems contain wire.
- Dust weekly with a soft brush or a hair dryer on cool.
- Keep out of direct sunlight.
- Fluff squashed petals with your fingers.

### Crochet clothing and hats

- Fold, don't hang.
- Wash after 4–5 wears unless dirty.
- Dry flat and ease back to the original size.
- Dry bucket hats over an upturned bowl.

## Reshaping (blocking)

Crochet that has stretched or skewed can often be saved.

1. Wet it with cool water, then roll it in a towel to remove the water.
2. Lay it out on foam mats or a thick towel at its original size.
3. Pin the edges with rust-proof pins.
4. Leave until completely dry.

For acrylic you can also use steam from an iron held a few centimetres away, as long as the iron doesn't touch the crochet.

## Dealing with pilling

Small bobbles where the fabric rubs are normal. Shave them gently with a fabric shaver or small scissors. Don't pull them.
